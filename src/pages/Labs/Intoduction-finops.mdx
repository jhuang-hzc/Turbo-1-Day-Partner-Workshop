Gen-Z Bank is a financial services platform focused on serving a millennial customer base. The bank recently completed a large-scale cloud migration, successfully moving their workload to AWS and Azure. However, this transition has resulted in significantly higher cloud spending and the monthly cloud bill has begun drawing regular executive attention of from the financial team. The CIO has formed a new FinOps group to address the issue.

FinOps is about making cloud spending visibile, manageable, and financially responsible. It’s not just technical optimization but also alaigning engineering decisions with real cost impacts.

In this demo, I’ll show you how the IBM Turbonomic platform helps the FinOps team at Gen-Z Bank control their cloud costs.

The FinOps Lab is divided into four sections:

- Getting a global view of multi-cloud costs and savings opportunities
- Rightsizing compute instances
- Optimizing storage volumes
- Leveraging cloud provider discounts

In the public cloud model, cost is directly tied to resource consumption. Organizations often overspend because workloads are overprovisioned, left idle, or poorly configured. By improving visibility and aligning engineering decisions with financial responsibility, organizations can reduce waste while maintaining performance and reliability. 

IBM Turbonomics platforms provides automated, data-driven recommendations that help FinOps teams ensure that resources are allocated efficiently and that the cloud spending remains aligned with business priorities.
